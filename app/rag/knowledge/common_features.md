# Common features across all four plans

- WellVision Exam every calendar year. Focus on eye and overall wellness.
- Essential Medical Eye Care available as needed ($20 per exam).
- Prescription glasses coverage: frame + lenses, with copay per plan.
- Contacts in lieu of glasses -- one or the other per benefit period. If a
  member uses their contact lens allowance, their frame and lens benefit is
  not available again until the next benefit period starts. This is the
  most common source of "why can't I get glasses too" confusion -- always
  explain the in-lieu-of rule in plain language, not just the term itself.
- VSP EasyOptions: the member can pick one upgrade per period (extra frame
  allowance, premium/custom progressives, light-reactive lenses, anti-glare,
  or extra contact allowance).
- VSP LightCare: an allowance for ready-made non-prescription sunglasses or
  blue light glasses, instead of Rx glasses or contacts.
- 20% off additional eyewear from a VSP provider within 12 months of the
  last exam. This discount is available even when a member has already used
  their frame or contact allowance for the period -- it's a good next step
  to offer someone who already used their benefit.
- 15% off Laser Vision Correction at contracted facilities.
