# Plan quick reference

The four VSP plan types members can be on.

| | Signature | Choice | Advantage | Enhanced Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Provider network | VSP Signature (Premier Edge) | VSP Choice | VSP Advantage | VSP Advantage |
| Exam copay | $10 | $15 | $20 | $15 |
| Frame allowance | $200 | $180 | $150 | $220 |
| Featured Frame Brands bonus | +$20 | +$20 | +$20 | +$20 |
| Contact allowance | $150 | $130 | $120 | $180 |
| Premium progressive lenses | $80-$90 | $95-$105 | $95-$105 | $95-$105 |
| Extra savings on other enhancements | up to 40% | 30% | 20-25% | 30% |
| Out-of-network exam reimbursement | up to $50 | up to $45 | up to $45 | up to $45 |
| Frequency | Calendar year | Calendar year | Calendar year | Calendar year |

## About individual plans (IP)

Individual plans use the same benefit structure, but the member pays their
own monthly premium (autopay by default). Billing questions are only
relevant to individual-plan (IP) members -- members on an employer-sponsored
plan do not pay VSP directly and should not be routed to billing.

Example IP member: David Chen, Enhanced Advantage, premium $34.78/month,
autopay on the 15th, last successful payment Sep 15, 2026.

## Out-of-network reimbursement notes

- Out-of-network exam reimbursement is capped by plan (see table above).
  A member who pays more out of pocket out-of-network only gets reimbursed
  up to their plan's cap, not the full visit cost.
- Members submit an itemized receipt to be reimbursed. Treat this as a
  documented but out-of-flow step -- do not build a submission form.
- Reimbursement typically processes in 30 days. A "Pending" claim older
  than 30 days is a reason to escalate.
